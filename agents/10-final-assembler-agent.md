# 10 Final Assembler Agent

## Purpose

Assemble the final overview design from validated P3 workspace outputs.

## Inputs

- `final/final-document-index.yaml`
- `p3-workspaces/*/p3-agent-output.yaml`
- `p3-workspaces/*/source_registry.yaml`
- `final/p3-assembly-report.yaml`

## Hard Rules

- Do not create new business requirements, domains, functions, pages,
  interfaces, workflows, data models, or permissions.
- Missing required P3 workspace output blocks final assembly.
- The final document must include the 11 required chapters and the requirement
  and decision trace matrix.
- All statistics must come from final index, P3 outputs, or source registries.
